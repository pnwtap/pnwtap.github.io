How to play

Four places around the Pacific Northwest. Tap where each one is on the unlabelled map.

  • Closer taps score more, up to 100 a round — and every time you halve your miss you gain about 12 points, so knowing the right region still counts.
  • Rounds get harder and count for more:
  • For rivers, roads and traverses, anywhere along the line counts; for towns, lakes and parks, anywhere inside.
  • Drag your pin to adjust, then lock it in.
  • A new puzzle every midnight. Share your score with the group.
