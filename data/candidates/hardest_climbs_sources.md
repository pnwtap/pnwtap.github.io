# Hardest boulder on the map — Event Horizon (added 2026-09-29)

Two independent researchers (top-down lists; area by area) and a skeptic who verified the winner and pinned it.

Row: Event Horizon (V16),climb,medium,"49.6834,-123.1505",,"Canada's first V16, a squat-start prow in the Grand Wall boulders below the Chief; Andy Lamb sent it in 2024 and Ethan Salvo repeated it in 2025.","type: boulder problem | grade: V16 (8C+) | style: boulder | rock: granite | first_ascent: 2024, Andy Lamb | region: Sea-to-Sky, BC",The hardest boulder problem on this map

## Verdict

High, about 90%. Event Horizon is the answer, but it is tied on grade, not alone at the top.

GRADE: the top grade in the region is V16 / 8C+, and nothing is graded harder.
- climbing-history.org's Canada list (checked 2026-09-29) has 0 problems at 9A and only 2 at 8C+: Event Horizon and Flower of Sulphur.
- Its USA list at 8C+ and harder (26 problems) has none in WA, OR or AK. The possibly ambiguous crags (Thunder Ridge, Secret Garden, Coal Creek) are all in Colorado, and Tahoe Mountain Road is in California.

WHY EVENT HORIZON HAS THE STRONGER CLAIM:
- Andy Lamb did the first ascent on 10 Feb 2024 after about 55 sessions; it was announced in July 2025 (Gripped, 16 Jul 2025).
- Ethan Salvo made the second ascent on 13 Jun 2025 after 100+ sessions. He proposed no downgrade, and nobody else has either.
- There is no third ascent on record.
- It is a boulder problem, not a route: climbing-history files it as a "Boulder problem at the Grand Wall Boulders". It is the low start to Room Service Low (V14), finishing on Room Service (V12).
- It looks intact. No broken holds have been reported, and the shared upper feature, Room Service Low, was still being climbed through Jul 2026 (Schubert Nov 2025, Tan, Ohuchi, Dau).

THE TIE: Flower of Sulphur (V16 / 8C+, Squamish, reportedly Paradise Valley).
- Hamish McArthur made the first ascent in May 2026. Gripped (22 Sep 2026) says it is still unrepeated, so its grade is a single-ascent proposal.
- Weaker claim than Event Horizon, but a real tie on paper.
- Flower of Sulphur is also Squamish-area, about 16 km north. Choosing between them changes the scoring very little.

LOCATION (why 49.6834,-123.1505):
- Mountain Project's Dreamcatcher page says to walk "from the East side into the little square between the huge granite blocks and look to your left", and puts it on the "North side of the Cacodemon boulder".
- Gripped says Dreamcatcher "towers above" Event Horizon on the same feature.
- OSM traces the Cacodemon rock (way 1330030628) with its north face at about 49.6834, -123.15056 to -123.15041. Another rock (way 1330030629) closes the square on the east and 11-bolt Rock (way 1330030630) on the north.
- I stitched Esri imagery at zoom 20 and overlaid the OSM outlines. The shadowed hollow against Cacodemon's north face is centred at 49.68343, -123.15052, about 4 m from the chosen point.
- Estimated accuracy is ±15 m.
- in_region check: True.

OTHER COORDINATES REJECTED:
- Researcher 2's 49.6833,-123.1502 is about 24 m ESE, on Cacodemon's east side, not in the square.
- Mountain Project's pins are coarse: The Room 49.68241,-123.1506 is about 110 m S (it sits on the trail-junction guidepost), and Dreamcatcher 49.68452,-123.1505 is about 125 m N.

MAP NOTE: the existing "The Chief" row (49.6839,-123.1417) is 636 m away, so the variety rules will space the two days apart.

## Alternatives

FLOWER OF SULPHUR: V16 / 8C+, Squamish area, reportedly the Paradise Valley boulders about 20 km N of town.
- Hamish McArthur, May 2026 (before 17 May). 1 ascent, unrepeated as of 22 Sep 2026 (Gripped). Ranked #83 on climbing-history's world list.
- Researcher 2's point, from the Kaya subarea pin, is about 49.8292,-123.1447 (±50 m).
- I could not independently confirm the sub-area or the point: Kaya blocked the request (Cloudflare) and climbing-history lists it only as "Squamish".
- Use it only if the owner wants "newest V16" rather than "hardest confirmed".

NEXT GRADE DOWN, V15 / 8C (all in Squamish; none can tie):
- The Singularity: same square as Event Horizon. Tim Clifford 2007, now 12 ascents.
- IMAX: North Walls, Ethan Salvo 2025.
- The Youngster's Roof: Salvo 2025.
- Chicken Nuggets: Jake Scharfman 2025.
- Tilde: Ashlu, McArthur 2026.

- Zazen: Apron.

DO NOT USE:
- "The Megg": claimed as V16 in 2023. The claim was false, and it is now Fool Me Once, V14.

OUTSIDE SQUAMISH (researcher reports; I only spot-checked that no WA/OR/AK problem reaches 8C+):
- Washington tops out at V14: The Penrose Step, Leavenworth.
- Oregon tops out at V14: Murder Mitten and Paper Halo, Smith Rock.
- Okanagan: V14.
- Alberta: about V13.
- Yukon and SE Alaska: nothing notable.

# Hardest sport climb on the map — Fight Club & Disbelief (added 2026-09-29)

Same method: two researchers (top-down lists; area by area) and a skeptic who verified the winner and pinned it.

Row: an "any of" row, because two routes share the top grade. Prompt "The hardest sport climb on this map".
- Fight Club, Raven Crag, Banff NP: 51.1612,-115.5896 (Mountain Project crag GPS, about ±150 m). Alex Megos, 2016, 5.15b/9b, Canada's first 5.15.
- Disbelief, Acéphale Upper Wall, Bow Valley PP: 51.0454,-115.1291 (Mountain Project crag GPS, about ±150 m). Adam Ondra, 2018, 5.15b/9b.

## Verdict

High that 5.15b/9b is the top sport grade in the region and that exactly these two routes hold it; medium that the tie is exact.
Neither route has been repeated (climbing-history.org shows one ascent each; no repeat in Gripped's archive to late Sep 2026),
so both grades are first-ascent proposals. Ondra called Disbelief "pretty high-end 9b"; Fight Club lost holds in 2017 and 2020.
Nobody has proposed a regrade of either, so the row is a tie.

Next tier (5.15a/9a+): Midnight Way (Squamish, 2024, BC's hardest sport route), Sacrifice (Echo Canyon, 2018), Queen Bee Direct.
Excluded: Semantics (top-rope only), Honour and Glory (now 5.14d), Drifter's Escape (trad), the Index open project (unclimbed),
Feed the Boss (Horne Lake; unconfirmed, and at most a tie).

Brave New World (Little Si, 5.14d/9a, 2016) stays correct as "hardest sport route in Washington"; region-wide it is two
sub-grades lower and ties about ten other 9a routes.

# Hardest trad climb on the map — Cobra Crack, Crack of Destiny & Family Man (added 2026-09-29)

Same method. Rule: pure trad (no protection bolts; bolted anchors are fine), ranked by consensus grade. On that rule three
routes tie at 5.14b/8c, so the row is an "any of". About 80% confident.

- Cobra Crack, the Cirque, Stawamus Chief: 49.6814,-123.1389 (MP route GPS; OSM crag node "The Cirque" 30 m away; ±30 m).
  Sonnie Trotter, first free ascent 23 Jun 2006; about 23 ascents. Consensus 5.14b (Wikipedia, Gripped 2024/2026, repeaters' 8c).
- The Crack of Destiny, North Walls, Stawamus Chief: 49.6865,-123.1374 (placed from route descriptions on the North Gully's east
  wall, across from the top of Angel's Crest; ±100 m; the MP area pin doesn't fit the descriptions). Didier Berthod, 23 Jun 2023;
  repeated by Herson, Harnden and Bailey; every source gives 5.14b/8c; all cams.
- Family Man, Skaha Bluffs: 49.4413,-119.5634 (MP Skaha area pin; no readable source names the sector; ±2 km, which barely
  matters at a 25 km scoring scale). Trotter, 21 Oct 2014, "soft 5.14b"; repeated by Harnden (2016) and Stu Smith (2024), no downgrade.

Excluded (bolts, or grade unconfirmed; all on the Chief within 0.7 km of Cobra Crack, so scoring would hardly change):
Drifter's Escape (proposed 5.15a, Herson 2025, two protection bolts; Herson: "I'm not claiming a 'trad' ascent"), Voight-Kampff
Test (5.14b/c, one bolt, unrepeated), The Shark (5.14b, starts on three bolts). Washington tops out at 5.14a (The Quad Crack,
En Passant on gear, both Index); Oregon at 5.13+.
