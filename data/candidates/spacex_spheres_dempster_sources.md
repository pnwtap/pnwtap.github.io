# SpaceX Starlink factory, Amazon Spheres, Dempster Highway — sources (added 2026-09-29)

Researched, then independently fact-checked. SpaceX: the only current SpaceX site in the region is the Starlink satellite
campus at Redmond Ridge (named without the town so the location is the test). Dempster: the line runs from the Klondike
Highway junction through the Yukon and on through the NWT stretch the map still shows; facts describe the whole highway.

## Identification notes (seattle)

SpaceX facilities in the game region (WA/OR/BC/AB/YT). I checked news, Wikipedia, OSM, the King County and US Census geocoders, and an FCC filing.

(1) CURRENT MAIN SITE, proposed as the row "SpaceX Redmond". This is the Starlink satellite development and manufacturing campus in Redmond Ridge Business Park (also called Redmond Ridge Corporate Center). It is in unincorporated King County, about 6.8 km ENE of downtown Redmond, and its permits are issued by King County. OSM tags seven buildings here: "SpaceX Starlink SE02" to "SE08". They are SE04 at 22908 NE Alder Crest Dr (w236843230), SE02 at 23020 (w534754622), SE03 at 23040 (w534754623), SE08 at 10700 231st Way NE (w534754628), and SE05, SE06 and SE07 on NE Marketplace Dr (w1058011034, w1058011035, w1133276576). Together they cover about 700 x 400 m.
- SpaceX moved in with a 40,625 sq ft lab leased in January 2017. In August 2018 it moved all its Seattle-area work into a three-building facility here.
- In 2021 it added Buildings 4 and 5 (124,907 sq ft). Wikipedia now puts the campus at more than 314,000 sq ft.
- SpaceX said in August 2025 that the factory builds about 70 satellites a week, and its 2026 S-1 filing gives the same figure.
- The row is a POINT on SE04 at 47.6932,-122.0352. That is the building at 22908 NE Alder Crest Dr, the address used in SpaceX's 2020 FCC filing and in business directories. The King County address point is 12 m away (47.69315,-122.03531).
- I did not use a ring because other tenants sit between the SpaceX buildings: Triumph Aerospace, a Verizon switching centre, a Microsoft data center and a self-storage. A hull would draw them as SpaceX, and scoring would be the same anyway.

(2) THE 2015 ORIGINAL OFFICE, no row. "SpaceX Seattle" at 18390 NE 68th St, Redmond (Census geocode 47.6673,-122.0963) had about 30,000 sq ft and opened in January 2015. According to Wikipedia and GeekWire (Oct 2018), SpaceX moved all its Seattle-area staff to Redmond Ridge in August 2018, so this is no longer a current site. Some online directories still list it; those listings are out of date.

(3) STARLINK WAREHOUSE NEAR WOODINVILLE. It is real and located, but I did not propose it as a row. Starlink leases 197,552 sq ft in Building A at 8131 W Bostian Rd, Woodinville (LogistiCenter at Woodinville, also listed as Woodinville Distribution Center).
- The lease was signed in April 2025 according to permit records reported by the Puget Sound Business Journal and W Report. ConnectCRE (Oct 2025) confirms SpaceX is the tenant. CoStar says the building handles logistics and parts machining.
- The space was formerly Precor's distribution centre: OSM w950107490 "Precor Distribution Center", centroid about 47.7963,-122.1261. The Census geocoder puts the address at 47.7956,-122.1263.
- I left it out because it is a leased warehouse with nothing public about it, it is about 13 miles from the Redmond row so it would score almost the same, and it is not an office.
- If the user wants a second SpaceX row, a sourced version would be: "SpaceX Woodinville", poi, hard, point 47.7963,-122.1261, facts "type: satellite parts warehouse". SpaceX has not confirmed the lease itself; the building is identified from permit records.

(4) I found no SpaceX offices or factories in Oregon, BC, Alberta or Yukon. There are only Starlink ground stations, which are not offices. SpaceX's Starlink user-terminal factory is in Bastrop, Texas.

AMAZON SPHERES: the three glass conservatories at 2111 7th Ave, Seattle, on Lenora St between 6th and 7th Aves at the foot of the Day 1 tower. The row is a point at 47.6157,-122.3395. That is on the central (largest) dome, inside the OSM building footprint w868099742 "Amazon.com The Spheres" (polygon centroid 47.61570,-122.33955), and it matches Wikipedia's coordinate exactly. The whole complex is only about 60 x 80 m, so a point is appropriate.

Height: Enclos (the facade contractor) and Wikipedia (citing the Washington Post) give the domes as 80 to 95 ft. Amazon's press release says the largest is "more than 90 feet tall", 130 ft across. So height_m is 29.0, which is 95 ft (28.956 m) and displays as "29 m (95 ft)".

## Dempster Highway

Geometry: OSM route relation 5386003 (Dempster Highway, network CA:YT, ref 5), fetched with Overpass ('rel(5386003);out body;way(r);out geom;'). All 33 member ways are highway=primary. They were joined into a graph at shared nodes, and the line is the shortest path from node 2696026377 (63.9905,-138.7514) to node 2662264235 (67.0513,-136.2032). The start node is shared with two Klondike Highway (YK 2) ways, and Yukon government km post 0 sits exactly on it. The end node is where the OSM ref changes from YK 5 to NT 8 at Wright Pass, 9 m from OSM's NT-YT boundary. The path uses every member node: 463.9 km over about 6,900 points, against a signed Yukon section of 465 km. It was simplified with Douglas-Peucker at 0.5 km tolerance using the game's local-projection segment distance, then rounded to 4 dp. Result: 115 points and 449.5 km. Fact-check against fresh OSM data (2026-09-29) reproduced the same path. Every raw OSM point is within 0.49 km of the simplified line, and every vertex is within 5 m of the road. The Yukon government road network (GeoYukon) matches the OSM alignment to within 17 m, and its km posts 0, 80, 82, 404, 406 and 464 lie within 0.4 km of the line. The line is open and scores as a line. It covers the Yukon section only; NWT Hwy 8 (relation 5384727) is left out. The row passes pnwtap.sheet.parse_locations with the bbox, region mask, categories and facts registry: 234 locations. Extended past the Yukon–NWT border through the stretch the map still shows (to about 67.83°N, past Fort McPherson and Tsiigehtchic): OSM route relation 5384727 chained from the border node, clipped where it leaves the region mask, Douglas-Peucker at 0.4 km (48 points, 187 km).

Notes: Scope: the line is the Yukon section only (Yukon Hwy 5), from the Klondike Hwy junction about 40 km east of Dawson City to the NWT border at Wright Pass (signed km 465). The facts describe the whole highway to Inuvik (736 km), the same way the Columbia and Yukon River rows use whole-river lengths. The full road can't be drawn because Inuvik is outside the region mask. However, the stencil's 0.9-degree margin does show NWT imagery along the road to about 67.83N, past the Peel River ferry, Fort McPherson and Tsiigehtchic. A tap on the real road there scores against the Yukon-only line: Peel ferry 65 km away scores 75, Fort McPherson 71 km scores 73, Tsiigehtchic 113 km scores 62. The Arctic Circle crossing (signed km 405) and North Fork Pass (the highway's high point, signed km 80-82, inside Tombstone Territorial Park) are both on the drawn section. Length: published totals are 734 (Milepost), 735 (Yukon News; the Yukon government road network measures 735.1 km), 736 (Wikipedia, ExploreNorth, dangerousroads), 737 (Yukon government travelogue; Wikipedia infobox 737.5) and 740 km (NWT Parks). 736 is the most common. High point: published figures for North Fork Pass disagree: 1,289 m (Wikipedia, uncited), 1,298 m / 4,229 ft (NWT Parks, internally inconsistent), 1,300 m (ExploreNorth), 1,328 m (dangerousroads) and 1,400 m (Yukon government travelogue, Milepost). Four independent elevation models put the road summit at 1,320-1,333 m: ArcticDEM 2 m about 1,330, Copernicus GLO-90 1,333, NRCan CDEM 1,327, ASTER 1,320. So the row uses 1,328 m. Use 1289 instead if you prefer to follow Wikipedia. The 'road' key carries the highway numbers, as it does on the I-90 exit road rows. No tagline: the genuine claim to fame (Canada's only road across the Arctic Circle) would give away the latitude on the prompt. The clue line reads 'gravel highway · 736 km long · tops out at 1,328 m'. Difficulty is medium, like Dawson City: the road is famous as the drive to the Arctic, and the 450 km line forgives an approximate tap. Hard is defensible for players who have never heard of it.
