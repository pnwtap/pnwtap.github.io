## Kaleetan Peak (peak, medium)

Geometry: 47.4625,-121.4782

The pin is the summit high point, unchanged from the proposal. It rounds the OSM summit node 349018659 (47.4624576,-121.4782337) to 4 decimals, about 5 m away. Other sources: peakbagger's lidar summit (47.462465,-121.478195) is 4 m away, Lists of John (47.46250,-121.47821) is under 1 m away, and the older peakbagger/NGS point that Wikipedia uses (47.462526,-121.47825) is 5 m away. The live GNIS record 1521553 puts the summit at 47.4623429,-121.4780696, about 20 m off the top. USGS 3DEP gives 6,207 ft at that point. Terrain check with USGS 3DEP elevations: the pin reads 6,253.8 ft, which matches peakbagger's lidar summit of 6,254 ft. It is the highest of 8 points sampled within about 15 m (N 6,220, S 6,236, E 6,185, W 6,185 ft), so the pin is on the summit. The region check passes (in_region True). I wrote the corrected row with the csv module to a temp CSV outside the repo and ran parse_locations(csv_text, config.BBOX, set(config.CATEGORIES), region_mask, config.FACTS). It parsed clean

## McClellan Butte (peak, medium)

Geometry: 47.4069,-121.6222

Summit point, rounded to 4 dp. I re-derived it from four sources, which agree to within about 20 m. (1) NGS SX1264, disk 'MCCLELLANS BUTTE 2'. Its NAD 83(1991) position is 47 24 24.95438 N, 121 37 19.92475 W (47.406932,-121.622201). The description says it is 'on the highest part' of the butte, near the NW corner of the summit. (2) The OSM natural=peak node 349018424 (gnis:feature_id 1522865) is at 47.4069016,-121.6222901. (3) The GNIS 1522865 point, from the geonames MapServer Landforms layer, is at 47.406745,-121.622109, about 18 m SSE. (4) I sampled USGS EPQS 3DEP elevations on a 13x13 grid of roughly 7 m spacing around the point. The highest sample is 1573.77 m at 47.40696,-121.62211, about 8 m from the row point. The row point itself reads 1573.19 m, and the GNIS point reads 1559.7 m, a little off the top. PeakVisor (47.406657,-121.622217) agrees.
