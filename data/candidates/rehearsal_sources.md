# The Rehearsal farmhouse — sources (added 2026-10-31)

Event row for the season 1 farmhouse of HBO's The Rehearsal (Angela storyline). Researched, then independently fact-checked.

Identification: an HBO publicity still of the house (S1E2 'Scion', credited 'Courtesy of HBO' in the AV Club review) matches
on-site 2022 photos of Clackamas River Farm detail for detail (siding, trim, porch brackets, door and transom, rail, roses); the
owners told a visitor the episodes were shot there (r/TheRehearsal, Aug 2022). No press article or HBO page names the farm. The
show itself names Eagle Creek as Fielder's home base (S1E4). Interiors may partly be stage sets (Vision Stages, Troutdale).

Public venue: active business at 26700 SE Samuels Rd, Eagle Creek (Oregon business registry; Wedding Spot listing; a bookable
Sniffspot dog field). The owners live in the house; the public visits by booking. Not on the National Register; '1890 Victorian',
'45 acres' and the Barlow Road line are the venue's own descriptions. The church-origin story is a Reddit relay only, so it is
not in the blurb.
